## Coordinated identity and policy

The byte-sized ability ABI has no unused ordinary ID. Store `0x6C`
(`ABILITY_HEALER`) and dispatch effective `ABILITY_HOSPITALITY` only for
Sinistcha `0x589` and Masterpiece `0x58A`. This is a storage encoding, with
actual entry HP healing and distinct name/description; ordinary Healer's
end-turn status cure is excluded on those two species. Other species retain
Healer. This deliberately supports canonical species only, not portable
Hospitality through random assignment or ability transfer to unrelated species.

DPE assigns Hospitality / NONE / Heatproof to both forms. CFRU owns semantic
battle behavior and presentation. UPR-FVX preserves those exact canonical
slots for inputs carrying the new identity, including raw NONE in slot 2,
and uses species-aware logging. The semantic Hospitality ability is absent
from unrelated random recipients; numeric Healer remains available there.
Older inputs with Weak Armor are not treated as implemented Hospitality.
Deploy these three candidates together; DPE alone would expose Healer and
would not implement Hospitality. No ABI expansion or other Gen 9 sync.

## Reference behavior

M-010's Showdown reference at
[`d849b220082e113d8a17303509fb44d420c543d5`](https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown/tree/d849b220082e113d8a17303509fb44d420c543d5)
provides `data/abilities.ts` Hospitality `onSwitchInPriority: -2` and
`onStart`: heal adjacent allies by `ally.baseMaxhp / 4`. `sim/battle.ts`
`heal` truncates positive fractions, enforces minimum one, and rejects
inactive/fainted/full targets; [`data/moves.ts` Heal Block](https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown/blob/d849b220082e113d8a17303509fb44d420c543d5/data/moves.ts#L8309) rejects healing.
Local implementation uses floor(base max HP / 4), minimum one, capped at
missing HP. The active partner must be alive/present and not Heal Blocked.
